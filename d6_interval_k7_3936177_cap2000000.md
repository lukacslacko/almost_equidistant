## Trust boundary

The only rejection mechanism is the existing outward-rounded
interval kernel.  It assumes correctly rounded IEEE-754 binary64 basic
operations and square root, expanding every interval endpoint with
`nextafter`.  It assumes macOS `libm` cosine endpoint error of at most eight
ulps; both directions receive eight `nextafter` padding steps and all interior
extrema are included.  Candidate nonedges remain unconstrained and may also
have unit distance.  Only a complete `KILLED` tiling is a conclusion;
`ABORT`, `UNRESOLVED`, and `INFRA_ERROR` are not rejections.

The v6 selection root separately accounts for 264 exact algebra/graph
rejections and three independently checked interval increments; no
non-`KILLED` interval status was used in constructing it.
