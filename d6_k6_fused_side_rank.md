# Fusing K6 side-rank bounds before block Hall

This exact layer rejects 13 graphs from the manifest-pinned 990-graph K6-only
residue, nine more than the preceding all-systems same-`Z0` layer.  Its new
ingredient is simple but materially stronger: ordinary zero forcing and
Lorentz-orientation inertia lower-bound the dimensions of the same two actual
side spans, so their lower bounds must be combined side by side before the
orthogonal block-support inequalities are applied.

## Exact fusion theorem

Fix a required unit `K6`, an actual zero-Lorentz-factor set `Z0`, and a
connected bipartite component `C=A union B` of `L-Z0`.  In the generic
Lorentz case, write

```text
U_A = span{u_x : x in A},       U_B = span{u_x : x in B}.
```

These are the actual Euclidean defect-vector spans in the putative
realization.  They do not depend on which lower-bound theorem is used to
study them.

The exact Gram-pattern/ordinary-zero-forcing argument gives

```text
z_A = |A| - Zf(G[A]) <= dim U_A,
z_B = |B| - Zf(G[B]) <= dim U_B.                   (ZF-side)
```

For a chosen generic Lorentz orientation, exact inertia gives

```text
i_A <= dim U_A,       i_B <= dim U_B.              (inertia-side)
```

Here the two orientations are

```text
A positive:
  i_A = |A|-n_-(I+Adj(G[A])),
  i_B = |B|-n_+(I+Adj(G[B]));

A negative:
  i_A = |A|-n_+(I+Adj(G[A])),
  i_B = |B|-n_-(I+Adj(G[B])).
```

Because `(ZF-side)` and `(inertia-side)` concern the same `U_A` and `U_B`,
each orientation necessarily satisfies

```text
max(z_A,i_A) <= dim U_A,
max(z_B,i_B) <= dim U_B.                           (fused-side)
```

The spans `U_A`, `U_B`, and every one-dimensional span generated by a `Z0`
defect vector are mutually orthogonal.  Each lies in the coordinate subspace
given by the union of its allowed defect masks.  Hence every subset `J` of
these at most eight blocks obeys

```text
sum_(block in J) fused_rank_lower(block)
    <= |union_(block in J) allowed_coordinates(block)|.   (fused-Hall)
```

The implementation checks every nonempty subset for both orientations.

This rule subsumes the two old component tests.  A passing fused orientation
also passes the inertia-only block inequalities because its ranks are no
smaller.  Its full-block inequality implies the old zero-forcing ambient
bound because its side ranks are no smaller than `z_A,z_B` and its coordinate
union has size at most six.  The converse can fail: separate tests can place
their large lower bounds on opposite sides and never add them.

### Lightlike case

The generic orientation formulas are not imposed when the Lorentz direction
is lightlike.  Instead, every vector in `C union Z0` is orthonormal.  The
implementation retains the exact separate alternative

```text
|C|+|Z0| <= 6
```

together with a matching into the allowed coordinate masks.  A component is
rejected only when both fused generic orientations and this lightlike
alternative fail.

The proof remains one-sided in the allowed supports.  Candidate nonedges are
unconstrained and may be unit; an allowed defect coordinate may be zero.
Enlarging actual supports to allowed masks weakens `(fused-Hall)`, so a
failure with the allowed masks is a valid obstruction.

## A strict corpus witness

Graph `122871`, seed

```text
[4,7,9,11,13,18],
```

with `Z0` empty contains the bipartite component

```text
[0,1,2,5,8,10,14,15,16,17].
```

The zero-forcing side lower bounds are `(2,3)`, for old total dimension five.
In the `A_positive` orientation the inertia side bounds are `(4,2)` and its
old block-support test passes.  Thus the two old systems pass separately.
But the fused bounds are `(4,3)`, requiring seven dimensions in a coordinate
union of size six.  The other orientation already has inertia bounds `(3,4)`
and also needs seven dimensions.  The lightlike alternative has ten
orthonormal vectors and fails.  This is a concrete exact instance where
sidewise fusion is essential.

The synthetic unit control in `test_d6_k6_fused_side_rank.py` isolates the
same phenomenon: with `|Z0|=2`, zero-forcing bounds `(3,1)` and inertia bounds
`(1,3)` each have separate total six, while their sidewise maximum `(3,3)`
has total eight.

## Independent verification

`verify_d6_k6_fused_side_rank.py` imports no production evaluator or K6
production kernel.  It uses the frozen independent reconstruction and then
implements fusion afresh:

- inertia is obtained from exact SymPy characteristic polynomials and Sturm
  root counts, rather than production rational congruence;
- ordinary zero forcing uses independent initial-subset enumeration and
  simultaneous closure, rather than production sequential forces;
- the sidewise maxima and every block subset are freshly enumerated;
- the lightlike condition and non-bipartite system use the frozen independent
  Hall-subset and actual-support implementations.

The serial command

```text
python3 verify_d6_k6_fused_side_rank.py \
  --output d6_k6_fused_side_rank_verification.json
```

recomputed all 990 graphs in 13.87 seconds and returned `PASS`.  It matched
eight core decision fields for every graph, all 13 first impossible seeds,
every exact failed fused-component record, every non-bipartite Boolean, all
654 archive rows, and the complete rejection set.  The independent positive
control also passes.
